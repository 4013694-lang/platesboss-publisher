PlatesBoss Publisher

PlatesBoss Publisher is a personal publishing tool used by a paper tableware factory owner in China to post his own factory videos to his own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The owner prepares a video, caption and hashtags, signs in with his own TikTok account, and publishes — no manual uploading in the TikTok app needed.
