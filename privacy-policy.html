Privacy Policy — PlatesBoss Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PlatesBoss Publisher ("the App") is a publishing tool operated by an individual content creator ("we", "the Operator"). The App lets a TikTok account owner publish his or her own videos to his or her own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. This policy explains what data the App handles and why.

1. Data we collect

Account information. When you authorize the App through TikTok Login Kit, we receive your TikTok basic profile information (such as your display name and avatar) and an access token and refresh token for the scopes you approved. We use this only to show you which account is connected and to publish on your behalf.

Content you provide. Video files, captions and hashtags that you prepare for publishing are processed by the App solely to upload and publish them to your own TikTok account.

We do not collect data about other TikTok users, do not scrape TikTok, and do not access your messages, followers or any content beyond what is needed to publish your videos.

2. How we use data

Data is used exclusively to operate the publishing function: authenticating your account, uploading your videos, publishing them with the caption you approved, and showing you the resulting post status.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising or analytics. Video content you publish is transmitted to TikTok through its official API, at your direction.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on the Operator's own server. Video files are processed transiently during upload and are not retained by the App after publishing, except copies the owner keeps in his own media library outside the App.

5. Retention and deletion

You may revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Manage app permissions). Revoking access stops all future publishing. To request deletion of stored tokens and account information, contact us at the email below; deletion is completed within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adult business use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
