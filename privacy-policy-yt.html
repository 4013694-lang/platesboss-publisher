Privacy Policy — PlatesBoss Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PlatesBoss Publisher ("the App") is a publishing tool operated by an individual content creator ("we", "the Operator"). The App lets a YouTube channel owner upload his or her own videos to his or her own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3. This policy explains what data the App accesses through Google APIs and how it is used.

1. Data we access

Your channel's basic information. When you authorize the App through Google OAuth, the App can read the name and avatar of the YouTube channel you authorized (via the youtube.readonly scope), so you can confirm which channel is connected before uploading.

Your own uploads. Video files, titles, descriptions and tags that you prepare are uploaded to your own channel through the YouTube Data API (via the youtube.upload scope), and the App reads back the status of those uploads.

Authorization tokens. Google issues the App an access token and a refresh token for the scopes you approved. These tokens are the only credential the App stores.

The App accesses only data belonging to the channel that authorized it. It does not access other channels or users, does not read comments or messages, and does not scrape YouTube.

2. How we use data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used exclusively to operate the upload function: confirming the connected channel, uploading videos you approved with the title and description you approved, and showing you the resulting video status. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular: data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing upload feature described above; it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by any human except as necessary to operate the feature at the owner's direction, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data obtained through Google APIs with third parties for advertising or analytics. Video content you upload is transmitted to YouTube through its official API, at your direction.

5. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on the Operator's own server. Video files are processed transiently during upload and are not retained by the App after uploading, except copies the owner keeps in his own media library outside the App.

6. Retention, revocation and deletion

You may revoke the App's access at any time in your Google Account security settings (myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services). Revoking access immediately stops all future uploads, and stored tokens are deleted. To request deletion of any remaining stored information, contact us at the email below; deletion is completed within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adult business use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
